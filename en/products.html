OptoLock® Fiber Optic Transceivers

OptoLock® is Firecomms' patented innovation featuring a snap-lock design — insert or remove POF fiber without any tools, dramatically simplifying installation. Compatible with standard AK-series POF connectors.

Full lineup covers 125 Mbps ~ 1 Gbps data rates, -40~85°C operating range, Class 1 laser safety certified, designed for industrial Ethernet and automotive applications.
