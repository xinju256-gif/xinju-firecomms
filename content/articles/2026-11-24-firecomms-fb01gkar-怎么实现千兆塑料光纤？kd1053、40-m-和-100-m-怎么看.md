body_en: >-
  The Firecomms FB01GKAR is an OptoLock® Gigabit plastic optical fiber
  transceiver. In the manufacturer's design, it works with the KDPOF KD1053 PHY
  to support a point-to-point 1 Gbps POF link as described in IEEE 802.3bv. Page
  1, Table 2 of the Firecomms FB01GKAR Revision Orig datasheet lists reference
  distances of 40 m at 1 Gbps and 100 m at 100 Mbps over step-index POF. Actual
  reach depends on the signaling rate and installation conditions.




  \## Can the FB01GKAR connect directly to a host controller?




  The FB01GKAR provides optical transmission and reception. The KD1053 PHY supplies the multilevel encoding required for Gigabit operation. Page 3 of the Firecomms datasheet specifies the use of this PHY in the 1 Gbps mode. Check the host-to-PHY interface first, then use page 2, Figure 2 to verify the connections between the PHY and the optical transceiver.




  Review three items before building the board:




  \- Differential signals. The transmit pair is TD+ and TD−. The receive pair is RD+ and RD−. Use Figure 2 to check polarity and differential routing.


  \- Power supply. Page 2, Table 3 identifies the 3.3 V supply pins for the transmitter and receiver. Follow the recommended circuit for filtering, decoupling, and shield grounding.


  \- RSSI. Pin 9 provides a received signal strength indication that reflects average optical power at the receiver. It can help track changes in the link. Confirm communication performance with packet or bit-error testing.




  \## What do the 40 m and 100 m distances apply to?




  Page 1, Table 2 lists 40 m at 1 Gbps and 100 m at 100 Mbps, both over step-index plastic optical fiber. For home or office installations, verify end-to-end communication at the intended rate and cable length. Then repeat the test with the actual termination and routing bends.




  The OptoLock interface accepts POF with a 2.2 mm outer jacket for bare-fiber termination. The datasheet specifies coupling to 0.5 NA POF. Check both jacket size and fiber specifications when selecting cable. Page 1 also lists a 650 nm RCLED and an operating temperature range of −20 °C to +60 °C.




  \## How do you validate a Gigabit POF link with the FB01GKAR?




  1. \*\*Confirm the target mode.\*\* Build a point-to-point link with the FB01GKAR and KD1053. Record whether it establishes and maintains communication in the intended mode.


  2. \*\*Test the installation conditions.\*\* Use the planned fiber, termination method, and cable length. Record RSSI, link drops, and packet errors.


  3. \*\*Document the test setup.\*\* Record the fiber, length, operating rate, and ambient temperature so that different cables or board revisions can be compared.

Firecomms FB01GKAR 是一款 OptoLock® 千兆塑料光纤收发器，按原厂方案与 KDPOF KD1053 PHY 配合，实现 IEEE 802.3bv 所述的 1 Gbps 塑料光纤点对点链路。Firecomms FB01GKAR Revision Orig 第 1 页表 2 给出的距离参考为：阶跃型 POF 在 1 Gbps 下为 40 m，在 100 Mbps 下为 100 m。实际可达距离取决于速率和安装条件。



\## FB01GKAR 能直接接主控吗？



FB01GKAR 负责光电转换。千兆模式所需的多电平编码由 KD1053 PHY 提供，原厂规格书第 3 页明确要求千兆运行时配合该 PHY。设计时先确认主控到 PHY 的接口，再按第 2 页图 2 核对 PHY 到光收发器的连接。



上板前核对三处：



\- 差分信号。发送侧用 TD+、TD−，接收侧用 RD+、RD−。按原厂图 2 核对极性和差分走线。

\- 供电。第 2 页表 3 列出发射端和接收端的 3.3 V 供电脚。布局时按推荐电路核对滤波、去耦和屏蔽接地。

\- RSSI。第 9 脚是接收信号强度指示，反映接收端的平均光功率。它可以辅助观察链路变化，实际通信仍需用报文或误码测试验证。



\## FB01GKAR 的 40 m 和 100 m 分别适用什么工况？



按规格书第 1 页表 2，40 m 对应 1 Gbps，100 m 对应 100 Mbps，两项都以阶跃型塑料光纤（Step-Index POF）为条件。做家庭或办公布线时，先用目标速率和实际线长验证端到端通信，再复测实际端接和弯折条件下的链路表现。



OptoLock 接口支持外护套直径为 2.2 mm 的 POF 裸纤端接，规格书列出的光纤耦合条件为 0.5 NA。选线时同时核对外护套尺寸和光纤规格。器件采用 650 nm RCLED，原厂标注的工作温度为 −20 °C 至 +60 °C，见第 1 页。



\## 上板后如何验证千兆 POF 链路？



1. \*\*先确认目标模式。\*\* 用 FB01GKAR 与 KD1053 搭建点对点链路，分别记录目标模式下能否建立连接和持续通信。

2. \*\*再验证安装条件。\*\* 用计划采购的光纤、实际端接方式和目标线长复测，记录 RSSI、掉链和报文错误。

3. \*\*保留可复测的条件。\*\* 记录所用光纤、线长、工作速率和环境温度，便于不同线材或板卡之间对比。
