  Choose between the Firecomms FB2M5KRR and FB2M5KVR based on what you need to
  monitor. Consider the FB2M5KRR with RSSI if you need to track changes in
  received optical power over time. Consider the FB2M5KVR with Signal Detect
  (SD) if you only need to know whether the received optical signal has crossed
  a detection threshold. Both OptoLock® plastic optical fiber transceivers use
  pin 9 for monitoring, but its function differs. Review the circuit and
  firmware for that pin before changing parts.




  \## What does the FB2M5KRR RSSI output measure?




  Pin 9 on the FB2M5KRR supplies an analog current related to the average optical power at the receiver. A load resistor from RSSI to ground converts that current to a voltage. An ADC can sample the voltage to track changes associated with fiber bending, end-face contamination, or assembly changes.




  \- Rev. B, page 2, Table 3 defines pin 9. Page 6, Figure 8 shows the typical response with a 10 kΩ load resistor.


  \- Keep the test pattern, duty cycle, and sampling conditions consistent when comparing readings. To estimate absolute optical power, calibrate the measurement on the target circuit and optical link.


  \- A falling RSSI reading provides a troubleshooting clue. Use the bit error rate, CRC error count, or protocol status to assess the effect on communication.




  \## What are the FB2M5KVR Signal Detect thresholds?




  Pin 9 on the FB2M5KVR provides a threshold-based SD status output. Rev. E, page 2, Table 3 defines the pin. Page 8, Table 7 lists the receiver thresholds.




  \- The typical SD assert threshold is −28 dBm. The typical deassert threshold is −31 dBm. The approximately 3 dB separation helps prevent repeated state changes when optical power fluctuates near the threshold.


  \- The same page lists a receiver sensitivity of −24 dBm. When SD is asserted, use the PHY link status or a bit error test to confirm that data communication is working.


  \- The −28 dBm and −31 dBm values are typical. For alarm design or production acceptance, check the minimum and maximum values in Table 7 and validate them in the finished system.




  \## What specifications do they share, and what should you check before swapping parts?




  Both transceivers accept 2.2 mm jacketed plastic optical fiber with plugless OptoLock termination. The nominal supply is 3.3 V, with a specified range of 3.0–3.6 V and an operating temperature range of −40 to +85 °C. Interpret the specified 10–250 Mbps range in the context of the encoding scheme. For example, 100 Mbps with 4B/5B encoding corresponds to 125 MBd, and 200 Mbps with 8B/10B encoding corresponds to 250 MBd. See FB2M5KRR Rev. B, pages 1 and 9, and FB2M5KVR Rev. E, pages 1 and 8.




  \- \*\*Check the pin 9 circuit.\*\* When replacing an FB2M5KVR on a board designed to read SD status, review the RSSI load resistor, sampling circuit, and firmware logic for the FB2M5KRR.


  \- \*\*Check the receiver optical power limit.\*\* FB2M5KRR Rev. B, page 9, Table 7 lists −2 dBm. FB2M5KVR Rev. E, page 8, Table 7 lists 0 dBm. Verify the strongest incident optical power expected on short or low-loss links. Both receiver tables specify 250 Mbps PRBS7 unless stated otherwise, with optical power coupled through at least 0.5 m of step-index POF with a 1 mm core and 0.5 NA.


  \- \*\*Validate the complete link.\*\* Check pin 9 readings, link status, and error performance with the actual PHY or FPGA, target data pattern, and shortest and longest link configurations. For the PCB footprint and application circuit, see FB2M5KRR Rev. B, pages 2 and 10, and FB2M5KVR Rev. E, pages 2 and 9.

Firecomms FB2M5KRR 和 FB2M5KVR 的选型取决于要监测什么。需要记录接收光功率随时间的变化，优先评估带 RSSI 输出的 FB2M5KRR。只需要知道接收光信号是否达到检测阈值，可评估带 Signal Detect（SD）输出的 FB2M5KVR。两款 OptoLock® 塑料光纤收发器都把监测功能放在 9 脚，但输出定义不同，换料时要重新核对这一路电路和固件。



\## FB2M5KRR 的 RSSI 能测到什么？



FB2M5KRR 的 9 脚输出与接收端平均光功率相关的模拟电流。在 RSSI 脚与地之间接负载电阻，可把电流转换为电压，再通过 ADC 采样，观察光纤弯折、端面污染或装配变化前后的接收光功率趋势。



\- 原厂 Rev. B 第 2 页表 3 定义了 9 脚，第 6 页图 8 给出使用 10 kΩ 负载电阻时的典型曲线。

\- 做趋势监测时，应尽量保持测试码型、占空比和采样条件一致。若要把读数换算成绝对光功率，应在目标电路和光链路上标定。

\- RSSI 降低可作为排查线索。要判断通信受影响的程度，还需查看误码率、CRC 错误计数或协议状态。



\## FB2M5KVR 的 Signal Detect 阈值是多少？



FB2M5KVR 的 9 脚是 SD 阈值状态输出。原厂 Rev. E 第 2 页表 3 定义引脚，第 8 页表 7 列出接收端阈值。



\- SD 置位的典型阈值为 −28 dBm，解除置位的典型阈值为 −31 dBm。两者相差约 3 dB，有助于减少光功率在阈值附近波动时的状态反复切换。

\- 同一页另列接收灵敏度 −24 dBm。SD 已置位时，仍应通过 PHY 链路状态或误码测试确认数据通信是否正常。

\- −28 dBm 和 −31 dBm 都是典型值。设计告警门限或做量产判定时，应核对表 7 的最小值和最大值，并在整机上验证。



\## 两款收发器有哪些共同规格？换料还要核对什么？



两款均用于 2.2 mm 外护套塑料光纤，采用 OptoLock 免插头端接。标称供电为 3.3 V，规格范围为 3.0–3.6 V，工作温度为 −40 至 +85 °C。原厂给出的 10–250 Mbps 速率范围需结合编码理解。例如，100 Mbps 数据经 4B/5B 编码对应 125 MBd，200 Mbps 数据经 8B/10B 编码对应 250 MBd。参见 FB2M5KRR Rev. B 第 1、9 页及 FB2M5KVR Rev. E 第 1、8 页。



\- \*\*核对 9 脚接法。\*\* 已按 SD 状态读取设计的板卡，改用 FB2M5KRR 时，应核对 RSSI 负载电阻、采样电路和固件判定逻辑。

\- \*\*核对接收功率上限。\*\* FB2M5KRR Rev. B 第 9 页表 7 列出 −2 dBm，FB2M5KVR Rev. E 第 8 页表 7 列出 0 dBm。链路较短或衰减较低时，重点验证最强入射光条件。两份接收规格表均注明，除非另有说明，测试采用 250 Mbps PRBS7，并通过至少 0.5 m、1 mm 芯径、0.5 NA 的阶跃型 POF 耦合光功率。

\- \*\*验证整条链路。\*\* 在实际 PHY 或 FPGA、目标码型以及最短和最长链路条件下，分别检查 9 脚读数、链路状态和误码表现。PCB 封装与应用电路可对照 FB2M5KRR Rev. B 第 2、10 页及 FB2M5KVR Rev. E 第 2、9 页。
