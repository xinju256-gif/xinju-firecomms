  \## What should you check before installing the sample?




  The FR50DHNR is the horizontal, non-inverting version of the FR50DxxR receiver family. The Firecomms Rev. P1 preliminary datasheet lists the model on page 1, the pinout and recommended circuit on page 2, and the horizontal package and PCB hole layout on page 7.




  \- Check pin 1 for data output, pin 2 for ground, and pin 3 for supply. Verify the existing board connections for pin 4 and retaining pins 5 and 8. Pin 4 is internally unconnected on the FR50DHNR. Check the manufacturer’s grounding guidance for the retaining pins.


  \- Confirm the 3.3 V or 5 V supply, supply tolerance, decoupling, and downstream input thresholds. Page 6, Table 6, gives the supply and output-level specifications.


  \- Verify the actual PCB hole positions, connector orientation, and board load. Use the pinout comparison for the board inspection and measure system timing on the sample board.




  \[Compare the AFBR-2624Z and FR50DHNR pinouts and specifications](https://firecomms-cn.com/en/articles/0c6a13b2cac38d03/)




  \## Why test the first frame after idle if continuous traffic works?




  Continuous traffic can pass while the first frame after idle still deserves a separate check. Table 6 on page 6 of Firecomms Rev. P1 lists pulse width distortion (PWD) and first-to-third-pulse distortion (PWDinit) separately. The PWDinit limits are −10 to +10 ns. Firecomms characterizes this parameter with a minimum optical input pulse width of 20 ns and identifies the first pulse as the worst case among the pulses tested. The same table specifies a maximum propagation delay of 50 ns for each output transition.




  Test continuous traffic, the first frame after idle, and the first communication after power-up. Record the shortest of the first few pulses, the downstream sampling window, and CRC errors. Check propagation delay for both output transitions. Log power-up and the first frame after idle separately because they begin under different operating conditions.




  \[Download the Firecomms FR50DxxR Preliminary Datasheet, Rev. P1 (PDF)](https://firecomms-cn.com/assets/pdf/fr50dxxr-rev-p1.pdf)




  \## How do you run a short- and long-link regression test?




  1. \*\*Reproduce the original behavior.\*\* Keep the transmitter, fiber, protocol, and supply fixed. Record the short-link errors and the longer-link baseline with the original receiver.


  2. \*\*Install the specified FR50DHNR sample revision.\*\* Keep the other conditions fixed and repeat the continuous-traffic, first-frame-after-idle, and power-up tests. Log CRC errors, output pulse widths, supply voltage, and test duration.


  3. \*\*Apply the project’s acceptance criteria.\*\* Use the system’s actual sampling window, permitted CRC error count, and required test duration. If the project has a BER requirement, record total bits and bit errors separately. Cover the supply and temperature limits required by the project.


  4. \*\*Save both regression results.\*\* Document whether the short link improved, whether the longer link passed, and which ordering code and sample revision were tested. This gives you a baseline for later lots or PCB revisions.

\## 上板前要确认哪些引脚和电气条件？



FR50DHNR 是 FR50DxxR 系列的水平安装、非反相版本。Firecomms Rev. P1 第 1 页列出了型号，第 2 页给出了引脚定义和推荐应用电路，第 7 页给出了水平封装及 PCB 孔位。



\- 核对 1 脚输出、2 脚地、3 脚供电，以及 4 脚和 5、8 固定脚在现有板上的接法。FR50DHNR 的 4 脚内部未连接，固定脚接地按原厂资料核对。

\- 确认接收器采用 3.3 V 或 5 V 供电，并检查电源容差、去耦和后级输入阈值。FR50DHNR 的供电和输出电平特性见第 6 页表 6。

\- 用实际 PCB 孔位、连接器插接方向和板上负载复核。已有的引脚对比可作为上板检查依据，整机时序则在样品板上测量。

\## 通信跑通后，为什么还要测空闲后的首帧？



持续发送时，链路可能全部通过，空闲后的第一帧仍值得单独观察。Firecomms Rev. P1 第 6 页表 6 将常规脉宽失真（PWD）和首 1–3 个脉冲失真（PWDinit）分别列出。PWDinit 限值为 −10 至 +10 ns，规格书以最小 20 ns 光输入脉冲表征，并指出首脉冲是所测脉冲中失真最不利的情况。该表也分别给出了高到低与低到高传播延迟，最大均为 50 ns。



验收时分别测连续报文、空闲后首帧和上电后的首次通信。记录前几个脉冲的最短宽度、后级采样窗口及 CRC 错误。高低电平转换的传播延迟都要核对。上电启动和正常供电下的空闲恢复应分别记录，因为两者的起始条件不同。

\## 怎样做长短链路回归，才便于确认样品表现？



1. \*\*先复现原方案。\*\* 固定发射器、光纤、协议和供电，用原接收器分别记录短链路报错与长链路基线。

2. \*\*换入指定版本的 FR50DHNR 样品。\*\* 保持其他条件一致，重复连续报文、空闲后首帧及上电测试。记录 CRC、输出脉宽、供电与测试时长。

3. \*\*核对项目验收条件。\*\* 按设备的实际采样窗口、允许的 CRC 错误数和测试时长判定。项目有 BER 指标时，额外记录累计比特数和误码数。再按项目要求覆盖供电和温度边界。

4. \*\*保留回归结果。\*\* 验收记录同时写清短链路是否改善、长链路是否通过，以及完整样品料号和版本。这样后续批次或板卡改版时可以复测。
