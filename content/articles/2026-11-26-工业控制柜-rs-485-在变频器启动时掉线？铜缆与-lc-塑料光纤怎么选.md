body_en: >-
  If RS-485 times out, drops out, or reports CRC errors during
  variable-frequency drive (VFD) startup, speed changes, or load transitions,
  \*\*start by checking copper cable routing, shielding, the ground reference,
  and termination\*\*. If the errors are concentrated on a segment near motor
  power cables or between cabinets and rerouting is difficult, evaluate LC
  plastic optical fiber (POF) for that segment. The fiber removes the conductive
  path through the communication medium. The electronics at both ends still need
  suitable power, signal levels, and immunity to interference.




  \## What should you check when RS-485 errors begin at VFD startup?




  Log error timestamps and check whether they coincide with VFD startup, speed changes, or load transitions. Then check the following:




  \- \*\*Cable routing.\*\* Look for long parallel runs with motor power cables. Temporarily route the affected communication cable separately and compare timeout and CRC error counts under the same operating conditions.


  \- \*\*Shielding and grounding.\*\* Check shield termination and the reference ground against the drive and communication equipment manuals. For links between cabinets, verify that the ground potential difference stays within the receiver interface’s allowable common-mode range.


  \- \*\*Termination and stubs.\*\* Check whether bus termination matches the cable’s characteristic impedance and whether stubs are too long. The TI RS-485 design guide illustrates a 120 Ω cable with a 120 Ω termination at each end of the trunk.




  See the \[Siemens SINAMICS Engineering Manual, July 2017, EMC Installation Guideline](https://cache.industry.siemens.com/dl/files/185/83180185/att_938805/v1/SINAMICS_Engineering_manual_V6.5_July_2017_external-e.pdf) and the \[TI RS-485 Design Guide SLLA272D, Rev D, pp. 2–3](https://www.ti.com/lit/an/slla272d/slla272d.pdf#page=2).




  \## Which segments should stay on copper, and which are candidates for LC POF?




  \- \*\*A multidrop bus within a cabinet.\*\* Keep the existing RS-485 topology if the corrected copper installation passes acceptance tests under the equipment’s operating conditions.


  \- \*\*A point-to-point segment between cabinets or near motor power cables.\*\* Consider converting just that segment to fiber if it has recurring errors, a substantial ground potential difference, or limited routing space.


  \- \*\*A two-wire, half-duplex bus that needs to extend between cabinets.\*\* Evaluate an RS-485 fiber converter suited to the existing network. Check transmit/receive turnaround and confirm that conversion delay fits within the protocol’s response timeout.




  \## Can the Firecomms FE50MLNR or FE50MLIR connect directly to RS-485 A/B terminals?




  RS-485 A/B terminals use differential signaling. The Firecomms FE50MLNR and FE50MLIR use TTL-compatible transmitter inputs and TTL-compatible push-pull CMOS receiver outputs. If the equipment exposes only A/B terminals, use an RS-485 interface circuit or a suitable fiber converter for level conversion and direction control.




  Both devices are \*\*DC-to-50 MBd LC optical transceivers\*\* supporting \*\*3.3 V or 5 V\*\* supplies and an operating temperature range of \*\*−40 °C to +85 °C\*\*. Both transmitters are non-inverting. With light present, the FE50MLNR receiver outputs high and the FE50MLIR receiver outputs low. Check the serial idle level and logic polarity in each direction when designing the board interface.




  \[Read the Firecomms FE50MLIR and FE50MLNR datasheet, Rev A, pp. 1–2, Tables 1 and 3](https://www.firecomms.com/contentFiles/technicalDocuments/FE50MLIR%20FE50MLNR%20Data%20Sheet%20Revision%20A.pdf#page=1)




  \## Can LC plastic optical fiber reach 50 meters, and how should the retrofit be tested?




  Table 2 on p. 1 of Firecomms Rev A lists \*\*50 meters\*\* as an application distance for step-index plastic optical fiber, subject to installation conditions. Table 7 on p. 11 lists a light-on peak receiver input range of \*\*−22 to −1 dBm\*\* under the conditions in the table notes. Use these checks for field acceptance:




  1. \*\*Measure the actual cable assemblies.\*\* Cover the shortest and longest runs, including LC connectors and installed bends. Verify received optical power and margin.


  2. \*\*Reproduce the conditions that triggered errors.\*\* Use the same polling settings and test duration before and after the retrofit. Log timeouts, CRC errors, and recovery times during VFD startup, speed changes, and load transitions.


  3. \*\*Test recovery.\*\* Disconnect and reconnect the fiber, and restart each end separately. If the design retains half-duplex RS-485, also measure the longest request-to-response time.




  \[See the receiver optical power and test conditions in Firecomms Rev A, p. 11, Table 7](https://www.firecomms.com/contentFiles/technicalDocuments/FE50MLIR%20FE50MLNR%20Data%20Sheet%20Revision%20A.pdf#page=11)




  \## What information is needed to evaluate a control cabinet retrofit?




  Provide the existing interface and protocol, bus topology, line rate, segment to be converted, minimum and maximum cable lengths, and the operating conditions when errors occur. Include timeout or CRC logs if available.

工业控制柜中，如果 RS-485 在变频器启动、加减速或负载切换时出现超时、掉线或 CRC 报错，\*\*先排查铜缆的走线、屏蔽、参考地和终端匹配\*\*。如果故障集中在靠近动力线或跨柜的一段，且现场难以重新布线，优先评估将这段通信改为 LC 塑料光纤（POF）。光纤段可切断通信介质的导电通路，两端接口仍需核对供电、信号电平和抗扰性。



\## 变频器一启动，RS-485 就报错，先查什么？



先记录报错时间，确认是否与变频器启动、加减速或负载切换同步。再逐项检查：



\- \*\*走线\*\*。检查出问题的通信线是否与电机动力线长距离并行。可用临时改线做对照，观察原工况下的超时和 CRC 错误是否减少。

\- \*\*屏蔽与接地\*\*。按所用驱动器和通信设备的安装手册核对屏蔽层端接及参考地。跨柜通信还应检查两端地电位差是否超出接收接口允许的共模范围。

\- \*\*终端与支线\*\*。检查总线端接是否匹配线缆特性阻抗，以及支线是否过长。TI 的 RS-485 设计指南以 120 Ω 特性阻抗线缆为例，在主干两端各匹配一个 120 Ω 终端。



布线依据见 \[西门子 SINAMICS 工程手册，2017 年 7 月版，EMC 安装指南](https://cache.industry.siemens.com/dl/files/185/83180185/att_938805/v1/SINAMICS_Engineering_manual_V6.5_July_2017_external-e.pdf) 和 \[TI RS-485 设计指南 SLLA272D，Rev D，第 2–3 页](https://www.ti.com/lit/an/slla272d/slla272d.pdf#page=2)。



\## 哪一段适合保留铜缆，哪一段适合改 LC 塑料光纤？



\- \*\*柜内多节点总线\*\*。若整改后的铜缆在设备运行工况下通过验收，可保留现有 RS-485 多点拓扑。

\- \*\*跨柜或靠近动力线的点对点段\*\*。如果这一段重复出现通信异常、地电位差较大，或者走线空间有限，可评估仅将这一段改成光纤。

\- \*\*需要跨柜延伸的两线半双工总线\*\*。可评估匹配原网络的 RS-485 光纤转换设备。确认转换设备能处理收发方向切换，并核对增加的延迟是否满足协议的响应超时要求。



\## Firecomms FE50MLNR 和 FE50MLIR 能直接接 RS-485 A/B 端子吗？



RS-485 A/B 端子使用差分电平。Firecomms FE50MLNR 和 FE50MLIR 的发射输入为 TTL 兼容逻辑，接收端为 TTL 兼容的推挽 CMOS 输出。若现有设备只引出 A/B 端子，需要通过 RS-485 接口电路或相应的光纤转换设备完成电平转换与收发方向控制。



两款器件均为 \*\*DC–50 MBd LC 光纤收发器\*\*，支持 \*\*3.3 V 或 5 V\*\* 供电，工作温度为 \*\*−40 °C 至 +85 °C\*\*。两款发射端都是非反相。接收端有光时，FE50MLNR 输出高电平，FE50MLIR 输出低电平。板级设计应按两个方向分别核对串口空闲电平和逻辑极性。



\[查看 Firecomms FE50MLIR／FE50MLNR 原厂规格书 Rev A，第 1–2 页，表 1 和表 3](https://www.firecomms.com/contentFiles/technicalDocuments/FE50MLIR%20FE50MLNR%20Data%20Sheet%20Revision%20A.pdf#page=1)



\## LC 塑料光纤能传 50 米吗？改造后怎么验收？



Firecomms Rev A 第 1 页表 2 将 \*\*50 米\*\*列为阶跃型塑料光纤的应用距离，并注明取决于安装条件。第 11 页表 7 给出的有光状态峰值接收功率范围为 \*\*−22 至 −1 dBm\*\*，适用条件见表注。工程验收可按以下步骤执行：



1. \*\*测实际线束。\*\* 覆盖最短及最长线束，计入 LC 连接器和实际布线弯折，确认接收光功率及余量。

2. \*\*复现原来的报错工况。\*\* 在变频器启动、加减速和负载切换时，用相同的轮询配置和测试时长，分别记录改造前后的超时、CRC 错误和恢复时间。

3. \*\*验证异常恢复。\*\* 测试断纤、重新连接和两端分别重启。若保留半双工 RS-485 接口，同时测量请求到应答的最长时间。



\[查看 Firecomms Rev A，第 11 页表 7 的接收光功率与测试条件](https://www.firecomms.com/contentFiles/technicalDocuments/FE50MLIR%20FE50MLNR%20Data%20Sheet%20Revision%20A.pdf#page=11)
