Firecomms — Pioneer in Plastic Optical Fiber

Firecomms Ltd. was founded in 2001 as a spin-out from the Tyndall National Institute in Cork, Ireland, and is a global technology pioneer in plastic optical fiber (POF) communication components. Based on its proprietary 650nm red-light RCLED / VCSEL technology, Firecomms developed the OptoLock®, RedLink®, LC and SMI transceiver families — making fiber-optic communication as simple as connecting a speaker cable.

Renowned for complete EMI immunity, IEC 60825-1 Class 1 eye safety, and automotive-grade -40°C to +85°C wide-temperature reliability, Firecomms products serve demanding environments such as automotive ADAS, medical imaging, industrial automation, renewable energy, smart grid and rail transit. In China, Firecomms is represented by Shanghai SIMACITY Electronics as its official authorized distributor.
